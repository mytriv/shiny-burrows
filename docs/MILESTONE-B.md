## Rules and modules

- New pure `core/discovery.ts`: row-major JSON-serializable packed unsigned 32-bit words, one bit per cell. Seed every occupied body cell; flood through cardinal empty neighbors up to two cell steps. Reveal the first solid boundary but never propagate through it. Recompute only after a body-cell change or changed terrain; discoveries union monotonically throughout the attempt. After movement/mining/hazards, expose the new actual local neighborhood. Restart/next level recreate visibility. Never consult camera, viewport, DOM or clock. Revealing solid cells does not change collision or rewards.
- New pure `core/deposits.ts`: classify existing authored finite values as small/medium/rich/rare, preserve stable level/coordinate IDs and exact-once credit. Clues are authored as dirt cells pointing to a genuine nearby richer deposit ID (bounded local range); renderer shows only a few embedded flecks and warmer soil in discovered clue cells while the source deposit remains hidden. No direction arrow, animated glitter, random hint or new collectible. Validate clue references, distances, soil and ore metadata.
- `model.ts`, `levels.ts`, `simulation.ts`: add compact discovery state, initialization and post-world-step update. Save-ready plain arrays and stable IDs only; no persistence transport or SDK. Keep campaign progress separate from attempt state. No new gameplay events needed for presentation.
- `TerrainView`: move existing terrain/detail batches into eight-row chunks; compare tile references and discovery words to rebuild only affected chunks. Do not draw undiscovered ore or terrain. Each chunk includes an opaque flat 32×32 fog batch at the existing front plane, not a postprocess. Hide distant undiscovered objects; always reveal occupied cells and adjacent collision hazards in Core. Fog uses the same compensated grid transform, with no collision geometry changes. Record rebuild counts, visible instances and fog cells for profiling.
- First Burrow: keep its recognizable shaft/main route and optional paid bridge; add a genuine left/right excavation fork and hidden side pocket with ladder return. Amber Hollow: add a hard-soil rich pocket and alternate side reward. Golden Depths: add high-value deep detours and maintain upgrade benefits. Keep IDs, quotas, free base-power routes and clear exits; author local hint data. All three layouts independently original.

V3-G supersedes V3-F's explicit Down+Dig, no-up-mining restriction and active-shaft-only ladders. Preserve assets/CSS, authored geometry/reward IDs/values, prices, upgrades, platform collision and fixed-step collision solver. No new dependencies or other milestones.

Drop suppression always takes priority over ladders until the full collider clears the dropped top. Idle ladders remain non-solid.

Downward excavation gently centers a supported avatar within its current column before a shaft exists (collision-safe normal horizontal microsteps, no teleport). Without the old pre-created anchor, an off-center mole could otherwise straddle neighboring floor and stall. This is disabled on platform cells/drop initiation so one-way platform boundary collision semantics stay unchanged.

Anti-ratchet gate: downward traces cannot add above the initial body anchor or the highest already-authorized rung. Full-rung climbing may put part of the body above that anchor; releasing or stepping sideways must not turn that overlap into successively higher free ladders. Reachable upward excavation can explicitly raise the bound after actual traversal. This preserves return paths while preventing climb/release flight.

Connected upward traversal also fills actually touched empty anchor cells up to the already-authorized ceiling, e.g. the final body cell above a freshly dug bank. This allows holding Up continuously to step out, without a release/repress trick. It never grows above the anti-ratchet ceiling unless a reachable ceiling block was mined.

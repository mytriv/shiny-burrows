## Delivered rules and tradeoffs

Player state keeps canonical destination occupancy plus a serializable visual segment. Release cancels future repeats, not the already committed cell. Falls have priority over air steering; after leaving a rung horizontally, the side step completes before the next downward cell attempt. Blocks are mined from a stationary canonical cell, with unchanged2-hit dirt, gold extra hit,24-tick cadence and180-tick healing. No new ladder generation path was introduced. Up additionally requires a target rung/connector or the existing bank exit; disconnected gaps cannot be climbed. One fresh platform drop can be buffered until the current step ends; pause clears that uncommitted request. Boulders use warned, bottom-first whole-cell descent and visual interpolation, preserving stacks/crush/fragile-floor rules.

The destination is authoritative before its interpolated sprite arrives: discovery/rewards/collision and danger at that cell take effect immediately. Mid-step retargeting and lateral air steering are intentionally excluded. Spawn validation rejects fractional content, and no old durable-save migration is required because persistent saves are not shipped. Pausing can freeze a visual transition between cells while logical occupancy remains canonical. Player pacing/playtesting beyond scripted regression remains future validation.

The actual fog implementation uses one analytic ShaderMaterial and per-instance cardinal/diagonal masks instead of the proposed alpha-texture atlas. Gradients darken only discovered boundary cells; fully hidden cells remain opaque and submit no ore. Its9px decorative plane is compensated in Y for the approved camera projection, so feathering aligns with the square front grid. Material/meshes are shared and dirty-chunk cached. No postprocessing, new dynamic light or per-frame geometry rebuild.

Visual changes: larger clay bevels within exact32×32×22 bounds, softer normal relief and warmer specular shading, fewer tiny loose flecks and chunkier shapes, larger embedded faceted nuggets, bright eyes/cheeks/smile and a fuller orange helmet within the existing20×26 visual bounds. Lacquered panels/buttons have thick warm rims/highlights; directions sit left, tools right, with≥44px targets. All HUD values remain actual gameplay data. No fake XP/backpack or unrelated props.
